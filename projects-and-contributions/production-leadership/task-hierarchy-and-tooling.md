# Standing Up Production Process for a Volunteer Indie Team

I joined The Decree, then a 5-person indie JRPG in preproduction, as their
first dedicated production hire. No task structure existed yet, and the
team was bumping against the limits of their free-tier tooling. The team
has since grown to 14.

I designed a task hierarchy covering art, design, engineering, and
narrative. Then evaluated tool options against what the team could actually
sustain: headcount nearing free-tier limits, need for recurring workflows,
low overhead tolerance on a volunteer team. Recommended a migration path
and set the workflow conventions the full team now runs on.

I also built the recruitment pipeline from scratch: candidate tracking, NDA
and contributor agreement workflows, interview process standards. Wrote the
onboarding documentation too. It covers how the team works, where things
live, and what's expected. New contributors are still being onboarded
against it.

The studio is in pre-production, working toward a playable demo.
